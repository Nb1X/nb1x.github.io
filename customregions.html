Join custom regions

Join custom & modded regions

Joining custom regions on mobile is easy.

1. Launch Among Us and wait until you reach the main menu.
2. Go back to your home screen and open this website.
3. Press the modded region you want to add below.

Adding custom regions on console is not possible.

Use a mobile device/PC to play on custom regions.

Manual install

  1. Copy this command (leave the quotation marks): 
    "%appdata%\..\LocalLow\Innersloth\Among Us"
    It should open the data folder.
  2. Press Win + R and paste the command.
  3. Download the regionInfo.json.
  4. Delete the regionInfo.json of the data folder.
  5. Move the new regionInfo.json file to the data folder.
  6. Launch Among Us and create/join a lobby with the region you want!
